3D Printing & Slicer Guide

Mode 1: Watertight Hollow Shell (Standard Print)

Generates a true manifold inner & outer shell with structural socket spokes. Ready to slice directly with standard settings:

  • Wall Generator: Arachne (2 to 3 Perimeters)
  • Infill: 15% Gyroid (for collar ring & spokes)
  • Seam Position: Aligned or Scarf Joint Seam
  • Supports: None Needed (Pendant spokes use 45° self-supporting struts)
Mode 2: Vase Mode (Spiralize Outer Contour)

Engineered with a 2.0mm recessed socket cutout at the base and a solid upper volume so your slicer prints a sturdy mounting plate on the bottom layers and spiralizes the shade wall with zero Z-seam:

  • Spiral Vase / Spiralize Outer Contour: ENABLED
  • Bottom Shell Layers: 6 to 8 layers (1.2mm – 1.6mm)
  • Top Shell Layers: 0
  • Outer Wall Line Width: 0.6mm – 0.8mm (even on a 0.4mm nozzle for rigidity!)
💡 Bulb Safety Reminder: Always use low-heat LED bulbs only (max 8W–12W LED) with 3D printed PLA or PETG lamp shades to prevent softening.
